# Surface brief: home page

Scope: home page (`/ru/`, `/kz/`, `/en/`), and the world it sets for the seven inner pages. Visitor mode: Persuade. Build path: code-led.

Audience and job: officers of DBK, Baiterek, Kazakh Invest and the Akimat preparing a memo; they take away the scale of the whole complex and its benefit for the region. Action: open the complex in figures. Proof: dated figures with units and sources, real geography, owner's site photographs (to be supplied), named management, Solico Group record.

Constraints: Solico green is the main colour; Sofood Kaz logo (file pending); Solico Group logo and "part of Solico Group" on every page; routes and JSON copy unchanged; Kazakh glyph coverage; no invented farm locations, figures or dates.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is one printed map sheet. Milk comes in from two regions, the complex stands in Konaev, and the legend is the table of figures. It refuses the corporate hero photograph with a stats row and cards.

OWN-WORLD: Solico green land as the ground of every page, deep green sheet margin, milk-white linework and legend panels, pale reservoir water, one road-yellow marker reserved for the complex. Condensed grotesque capitals for labels, tabular numerals. Components are map furniture: margin bar, legend box, scale bar, place labels, sheet numbers. Status is a mark: solid built, hatch under construction, outline planned.

STORY: The visitor sees where the milk comes from and where the complex stands, reads the figures in the legend, then follows the scale down from region to city to site plan to the phase being built, and leaves with quotable numbers.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed green map of the Almaty and Zhetisu regions inside a white sheet border. Margin bar on top with logo, endorsement, navigation, language. Headline on the land, upper left, about 76 px. Konaev marker with yellow label right of centre. Legend box lower left with three figure rows and the primary action. Scale bar lower right.

FORM: Supply map sheet, position 6 of 7 on the grounded list; seed:5943779da095f836. Signature interaction: scroll steps the map scale (region, city, site plan, current phase), each step a settled sheet; under reduced motion the four sheets are static sections. Raises: tabular legend rows with unit, date, source; state by mark not hue; one recognisable place across scales.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
